PARTICIPANT REGISTRATION & APPROVAL — V31

NEW FLOW
1. New participant enters name, designation, place of posting, mobile number, email and password.
2. Firebase Authentication creates an Email/Password account and sends a verification email.
3. A draft profile is retained under participantRegistrationDrafts/{uid}; it is not shown to the Administrator until email verification succeeds.
4. On return to student.html after verification (or after verified sign-in), the profile is submitted under participants/{uid} with status=pending.
5. Administrator reviews Participant Registration Requests in the Host Portal's Administrator approval modal and can approve, reject, revoke, or delete the request.
6. Only email-verified accounts with participants/{uid}/status=approved can open the Participant Dashboard.
7. Logout signs out but does not delete the Firebase account or participant profile.
8. Other Examination Results and Hindi Pakhwada 2026 Result shortcuts are displayed immediately below the registration/login form and remain public.

FIREBASE SETUP REQUIRED
A. Firebase Console > Authentication > Sign-in method: enable Email/Password.
B. Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains: ensure the GitHub Pages domain (for example, YOUR-USERNAME.github.io) is listed.
C. Authentication > Templates > Email address verification: customize sender/name/message if desired. The standard Firebase verification action link is used.
D. Publish the included database.rules.json in Realtime Database > Rules. It adds participant and registration-draft access rules while retaining the existing rules.
E. In Firebase Authentication, verify that the project's email verification template is enabled and test with an actual mailbox.

SECURITY NOTES
- Email verification proves control of the email inbox; it does not prove the person's real-world identity. Administrator approval remains required.
- Participant profile approval is stored in Realtime Database and cannot be self-changed by the participant under these rules.
- Deleting a participant request record does not delete the Firebase Authentication user account. If permanent account deletion is required, use Firebase Console > Authentication > Users.
- Existing host approval remains separate. Do not replace the Firebase configuration.
