WORLD OF EXAM – V70 CHANGE NOTES

1. Detailed Result popup
- View Detailed Result now opens a centred modal dialog instead of inserting the long review into the assignment card.
- The modal has its own vertical scrolling area.
- Close button, backdrop click, and Escape key close the dialog.
- Closing restores the participant to the underlying page without changing result data.

2. Downloaded detailed result PDF
- Status label is placed on its own header line rather than sharing the question text line.
- Question, participant answer, and correct answer are measured and wrapped separately.
- Card height is calculated from the wrapped line counts with additional vertical spacing.
- Page breaks are checked against the calculated card height.

Scope:
- Changes are limited to allotted-tests.html and allotted-tests.js, plus this note.
- No Firebase rules, database paths, result calculations, or saved participant records were changed.
