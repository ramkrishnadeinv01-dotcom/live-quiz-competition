VERSION 49 — FRESH RE-ALLOTMENT & ASSIGNMENT ARCHIVING

Host Portal
- Re-allotting the same category + examination to the same participant now prompts for confirmation.
- OK creates a new unique assignment ID and generation, marks earlier records as previous, and leaves prior attempts/results intact.
- Cancel creates no new assignment and leaves the earlier record unchanged.
- Host Delete now archives the assignment (status ARCHIVED) rather than physically deleting its history.

Participant Portal
- Archived assignments are hidden from active Pending/Completed/Results lists.
- A fresh Olympiad allotment only counts attempts linked to its own assignment ID; attempts from an earlier allotment do not mark it completed.
- MCQ/General submissions are only associated with a fresh allotment when submittedAt is on/after its assignedAt timestamp.
- Participant Delete Completed Assignment now archives the record instead of physically deleting it.
- Previous submitted records remain available in the assignment history in Firebase; a fresh allotment is shown as a new pending task.

Firebase rules
- database.rules.json has the participant assignment write condition extended to allow status ARCHIVED for an already submitted assignment.
